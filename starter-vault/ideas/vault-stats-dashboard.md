A small panel that charts notes-added-per-week, split by type. The interesting question is
whether `lessons/` grows during heavy project work or only after it — which would say
something about when capture actually happens. Everything needed is already in frontmatter
(`updated`, `type`); no new data collection required. Worth starting once the vault is big
enough that the answer isn't obvious by eye.
